# Positioning and strategy

Decided 2026-09-13. Change it when reality disagrees — this is a working
position, not a monument.

## What we are

Creative production for DTC brands and the agencies that serve them. Static ad
design, short-form motion, email design. Delivered by a vetted African creative
roster, managed end to end by us.

## The three real advantages

**1. Operator proof, not borrowed portfolio.** We run TOREVO — a live Shopify
Advanced store with real ad spend, real Klaviyo flows, real creative testing. We
can show our own numbers, our own creative, our own losses. Almost no production
studio can say that, and it is the exact credibility the fake-portfolio scheme
tries to counterfeit. We own ours.

**2. Cost base.** A senior designer in Lagos earns excellent money at rates a UK
agency treats as rounding error. That produces 70–75% gross margins against the
55–60% a UK-based studio can manage. This is the same arbitrage that built the
Indian IT industry, and it is entirely legitimate on one condition, which is
non-negotiable: **pay above local market, in full, on time, every time.** A
roster that trusts us is the asset; a roster that doesn't is a rotating cast of
strangers and there is no business in that.

**3. Timezone.** WAT is GMT+1 — a full working-day overlap with the UK and EU.
That is a genuine advantage over an Indian or Filipino studio and it should be
said out loud in every pitch. US East Coast works too (we're 5 hours ahead;
their morning is our afternoon).

## The beachhead: UK/EU agency white-label

Not direct-to-brand first. Agencies first, and deliberately.

A UK agency buying overflow production has no credibility objection, no
procurement process, and no opinion about where we sit. They care about quality
and reliability, full stop. A DTC brand founder handing a stranger $5k/month
cares about all of it. So the agencies are reachable now and the brands are
reachable after we have three case studies.

The trade is margin: white-label sits low on the margin ladder and gets
commoditised if we let it. Accept that for the first two or three relationships,
and treat every one as a case-study factory rather than the destination.

**Sequence:** 2–3 agency relationships → 3 case studies with real numbers →
DTC direct at 2× the price → productised fixed-scope offers.

## Pricing

| | Monthly | Contractor cost | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agency white-label | $2,500–3,000 | ~$700 | 72% |
| DTC direct (first clients) | $4,000–6,000 | ~$1,100 | 73% |
| DTC direct (with case studies) | $8,000–12,000 | ~$2,000 | 75% |

Margin floor raised to 55% for this cost base — at these numbers, anything under
55% means something has gone wrong with scope, not with pricing.

## Targets

$6,000/month in new retainers. Three agency relationships and one direct brand
gets there. Concentration cap 25% while the base is small, tightening to 20% at
six clients.

## What we do not do

No ghost job posts to harvest portfolios. No showing a freelancer's work as
ours. Both are illegal, and both would burn the roster — which in a business
whose only compounding asset is roster trust, is the one unrecoverable move. The
operator proof above is why we don't need either.
