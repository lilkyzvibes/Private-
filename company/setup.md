# Setup: the two things that block outreach

Everything else is ready. These two need money and a decision, so they're yours.

## 1. Domain and email — blocking, ~$50/year

Cold email from a gmail.com address gets close to a zero reply rate and
contradicts the positioning in the first pixel a recipient sees. This has to
exist before a single message goes out.

- Domain: pick and register (Namecheap, ~$12/yr). Check availability for the
  working name or your own.
- Google Workspace: ~$7/user/month for `you@yourdomain.com`.
- Warm it up: send low volume for the first two weeks (10–20/day) so the domain
  builds sending reputation before real volume.

## 2. Getting paid — blocking, and the bigger one

UK and US clients paying a Nigerian entity is the single largest friction in
this plan. Two routes:

**Route A — Payoneer or Wise Business.** Fast, cheap, works this week. You get
receiving accounts in GBP/USD/EUR. The invoice still reads as foreign, which
costs you some percentage of deals at the enterprise end but essentially none at
the agency-overflow end.

**Route B — US LLC + Mercury.** Wyoming or Delaware LLC via Firstbase or Doola,
roughly $300–500 setup plus ~$100/year registered agent, then a Mercury business
account. Your invoice reads as a US company, which materially raises close rates
with US clients and removes procurement friction with larger UK ones. Takes 2–4
weeks. Also creates a US tax filing obligation — worth twenty minutes with an
accountant before committing.

**Recommendation: start with Route A this week and open Route B in parallel.**
The agency-overflow beachhead doesn't need Route B, so it shouldn't hold up
outreach — but by the time direct DTC clients are in the pipeline, you'll want
it done.
