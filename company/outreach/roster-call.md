## Why each line is there

**"Roster call, not a staff role"** — kills the resentment that comes from
people thinking they applied for a job. That resentment is what makes
freelancers warn each other off you.

**Naming the rate** — filters out everyone whose time you'd waste and everyone
who'd waste yours. Never post a rate-free call.

**Paid test** — $150 is the best money in the business. It filters for
seriousness, it's the only reliable predictor of real-brief behaviour, and it is
the exact opposite of harvesting free labour. Freelancer communities talk, and
this is what they talk about.

**"We'll ask you first and credit you"** — this is the promise that makes us the
place good people come back to. Get the portfolio release signed at onboarding
so it's a formality rather than a scramble.

**"Net 30, every time, no exceptions"** — then honour it even in a month when a
client pays us late. The roster is the only compounding asset we have.
