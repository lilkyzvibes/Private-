# Outreach — UK agency white-label (the beachhead)

Four targets, drafted and ready. Send order is top to bottom; these are ranked
by how likely they are to actually need overflow capacity.

**Who to contact:** Head of Production, Operations Director, or Creative
Operations — not the founder and not new business. Find them on LinkedIn, then
guess the email pattern (`first@`, `first.last@`) and verify with Hunter or
Neverbounce (free tiers cover this volume).

**Rule for all four:** one message, one follow-up 10 days later, then leave it.
Overflow relationships convert on *their* timeline — the week they oversell —
not ours. The whole goal is to be in the file when that week arrives.

They have their own studio, which makes them a harder sell but a much better
client when it lands — agencies with in-house teams oversell that team
constantly, and they already understand production.

## The offer to have ready when one replies

- **Pilot**: one batch at project rate, ~$400–600, so they can test the standard
  with no commitment. This is the close — say yes to it fast.
- **Then**: $2,500–3,000/month for a standing allocation.
- Never quote hourly. Quote per batch or per month.
- The pilot is where the case study comes from. Ask permission to reference the
  work (anonymised as "a UK performance agency") the moment it lands well.
