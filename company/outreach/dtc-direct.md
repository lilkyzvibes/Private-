**Hold these until there are 2–3 agency case studies.** Sending now wastes the

one shot. The list is here so it's ready, not so it goes out today.

**Who to contact:** Head of Growth, Performance Marketing Manager, or Creative
Lead. Not the CMO at this size — too far up.

**Before sending any of these**, actually look at their current ads. Name a
specific concept in the first line or don't send it. The whole difference
between a 2% and a 15% reply rate is that one sentence.

## Template

> **Subject: your {product line} ads**
>
> Hi {First} — went through what {Brand} is running right now. {Specific
> observation: the concept doing the heavy lifting, how long it's been live, what
> the rest of the set looks like relative to it.}
>
> That's usually a volume problem rather than a targeting one — not enough new
> concepts entering rotation to replace the fatiguing ones.
>
> We produce 20+ new concepts a month against a brand's proven angles. We also
> run our own DTC store, so this comes from doing it rather than theorising
> about it.
>
> Want me to send three concepts for {product}, free, so you can see the
> standard?

## The free-concepts move

Sending three real concepts unprompted is the highest-converting thing in the
whole playbook, and almost nobody does it because it costs real work up front.
Do it for any brand worth $5k/month or more — which is all four of these.

With Arcads in the stack the marginal cost of three concepts is close to zero.
That is a genuine structural edge over a studio that has to pay a designer to
speculate. Use it.
