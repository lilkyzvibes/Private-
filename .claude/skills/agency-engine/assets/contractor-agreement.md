# Independent Contractor Agreement — template

> Drafting starting point, not legal advice. Have a lawyer review once before
> first use — worker classification and IP assignment are the two clauses worth
> paying to get right. **Must be signed before any brief is sent.**

**INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT**

Between **{Agency Legal Name}** of {address} ("Agency") and **{Contractor Name}**
of {address} ("Contractor"), dated {date}.

### 1. Engagement
Agency may offer Contractor project work from time to time. Contractor is under
no obligation to accept any offer, and Agency is under no obligation to offer
work. Each accepted project will be described in a written brief specifying
deliverables, deadline, and fee, and is governed by this Agreement.

### 2. Independent contractor status
Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, or agent of
Agency. Contractor:

- controls the manner, method, and hours of performing the work;
- provides their own equipment, software, and workspace;
- is free to work for other clients, including Agency's competitors, at any time;
- is responsible for their own taxes, insurance, and benefits; and
- may subcontract only with Agency's prior written consent.

Agency specifies **what** is to be delivered and **when**; it does not direct how
Contractor performs the work.

### 3. Fees and payment
Fees are agreed per project in writing before work begins. Contractor invoices on
delivery and acceptance. Agency pays **net thirty (30) days** from acceptance.
Contractor acknowledges that Agency's payment terms reflect Agency's own
collection cycle.

### 4. Intellectual property assignment
Contractor **assigns to Agency** all right, title, and interest, including all
copyright, in all work product created under this Agreement, effective on
creation. Contractor waives any moral rights in the work product to the extent
permitted by law, and agrees to execute any further documents reasonably needed
to perfect this assignment.

Contractor warrants that all work product is original, that Contractor has the
right to assign it, and that any third-party assets incorporated (fonts, stock,
music, footage) are **licensed for commercial and paid-media use by Agency's
client**. Contractor will identify all such assets and their licences on delivery.

### 5. Confidentiality
Contractor will keep confidential all information about Agency, Agency's clients,
and their businesses, and will not disclose the identity of Agency's clients
without written consent. This survives termination by three (3) years.

### 6. Non-circumvention
During the engagement and for **twelve (12) months** afterwards, Contractor will
not directly or indirectly solicit or accept work from any Agency client that
Contractor was introduced to through Agency, other than through Agency, without
Agency's prior written consent.

This does not restrict Contractor from working with any client Contractor already
had a relationship with before the introduction, or from working in the industry
generally.

### 7. Portfolio
Contractor may display work product in Contractor's personal portfolio, with
attribution to Agency, unless the work is marked confidential. Agency's right to
display Contractor's work is covered by a separate Portfolio Release.

### 8. Termination
Either party may end this Agreement at any time on written notice. Termination
does not affect projects in progress, which will be completed and paid for, or
clauses 4, 5, and 6, which survive.

### 9. General
Governed by the laws of {jurisdiction}. Entire agreement on this subject.
Amendments in writing.
