**MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is made on {date} between **{Agency Legal Name}**, {entity type}
of {address} ("Agency"), and **{Client Legal Name}**, {entity type} of {address}
("Client").

### 1. Services
Agency will provide creative production services as described in one or more
Statements of Work ("SOW") executed by both parties and incorporated into this
Agreement. Each SOW will specify deliverables, volume, fees, and term. Where an
SOW conflicts with this Agreement, the SOW controls for that engagement.

### 2. Delivery through contractors
Client acknowledges that Agency delivers services through a vetted network of
independent contractors. Agency remains solely responsible to Client for all
deliverables, quality, and timelines, and Client's sole contractual relationship
is with Agency.

### 3. Fees and payment
Fees are set out in the applicable SOW. Retainer fees are **invoiced monthly in
advance** and payable within **fifteen (15) days** of invoice date. Late amounts
accrue interest at {1.5}% per month. Agency may suspend services on accounts more
than thirty (30) days overdue, on written notice.

### 4. Term and termination
This Agreement begins on {date} and continues until terminated. Either party may
terminate an SOW on **thirty (30) days'** written notice. Client remains liable
for fees for work performed and work in progress through the effective
termination date. Fees for the final month are not refundable pro rata.

### 5. Intellectual property
Upon **full payment** for the applicable deliverables, Agency assigns to Client
all right, title, and interest in the final deliverables produced under the
relevant SOW.

Agency retains ownership of its pre-existing materials, tools, templates,
processes, and know-how, and of any working files, drafts, and rejected concepts,
and grants Client a non-exclusive licence to such pre-existing materials only to
the extent embedded in the deliverables and necessary for Client's use of them.

Third-party assets (fonts, stock imagery, music, footage) are licensed rather than
assigned; Agency will identify such assets and the scope of their licences on
delivery.

### 6. Portfolio rights
Agency may display deliverables produced under this Agreement in its portfolio
and marketing materials, with attribution to Client, unless Client notifies
Agency in writing that specific work is confidential. Agency will honour any such
notice.

### 7. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information in confidence and use
it only to perform this Agreement. This obligation survives termination by three
(3) years. It does not apply to information that is public, independently
developed, or lawfully received from a third party.

### 8. Non-solicitation
During the term and for **twelve (12) months** afterwards, Client will not
directly engage, or attempt to engage, any contractor introduced to Client by
Agency, other than through Agency, without Agency's prior written consent.

### 9. Warranties and disclaimer
Agency warrants that services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike
manner and that, to its knowledge, deliverables will not infringe the
intellectual property rights of any third party. Except as stated, services are
provided without further warranty of any kind.

### 10. Limitation of liability
Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. Each
party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees
paid by Client in the **three (3) months** preceding the claim. Nothing here
limits liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, or anything that cannot lawfully
be limited.

### 11. Independent contractors
The parties are independent contractors. Nothing here creates a partnership,
joint venture, agency, or employment relationship.

### 12. General
This Agreement is governed by the laws of {jurisdiction}. It constitutes the
entire agreement between the parties on this subject and supersedes prior
discussions. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties. Neither
party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, except to a
successor in a merger or sale of substantially all assets.
