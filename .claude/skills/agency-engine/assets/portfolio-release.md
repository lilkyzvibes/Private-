# Portfolio Release — template

> Get this signed at onboarding, alongside the contractor agreement — not at 11pm
> before a pitch. It is the document that makes it lawful to show a roster
> member's work to a prospective client, and it takes thirty seconds to sign when
> nothing is at stake.

**PORTFOLIO USE RELEASE**

Between **{Agency Legal Name}** ("Agency") and **{Contractor Name}**
("Contractor"), dated {date}.

### 1. Grant
Contractor grants Agency a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to reproduce and
display the works listed below (the "Works") in Agency's:

- client pitches, proposals, and presentations;
- website, portfolio, and marketing materials;
- social media and case studies.

### 2. Attribution
Agency will attribute the Works to Contractor wherever they are displayed, in
substantially the form: **"{Work title} — by {Contractor Name}, {Agency} network"**.

Agency will **not** represent the Works as having been created by Agency or by
any other person.

### 3. Works covered

| Work | Description | Original client (if any) | Restrictions |
|---|---|---|---|
| {title} | {description} | {client or "personal project"} | {none / anonymise client / do not show publicly} |
| | | | |

Contractor confirms that Contractor owns or controls the rights in each Work
listed, and that displaying it as described does not breach any obligation
Contractor owes to a third party. Where a Work was made for another client under
a confidentiality obligation, Contractor will say so above and Agency will
anonymise or exclude it.

### 4. Withdrawal
Contractor may withdraw any Work on written notice. Agency will remove it from
materials within thirty (30) days, and is not required to recall materials
already distributed.

### 5. No obligation
This release does not oblige Agency to offer Contractor work, or Contractor to
accept it.
